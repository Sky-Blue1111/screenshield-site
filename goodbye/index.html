If something went wrong

  • A site looked broken: you can pause ScreenShield on just that site from the popup, instead of removing it.
  • It hid too much: untick the kinds of information you don't need in the settings, or choose Ignore on a single item after a scan.
  • It didn't hide something: see common fixes.
  • Price: the free plan has no limits on blurring by hand.

Cancel a subscription

Removing the extension doesn't cancel a subscription. To cancel, reinstall ScreenShield, open the popup and choose Manage subscription, or contact us with the email address you paid with.
