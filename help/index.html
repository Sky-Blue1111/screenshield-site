Help and guides

Everything you need to keep private information off your shared screen.

Get started in a minute

  1. Pin ScreenShield. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to ScreenShield, so it's always one click away.
  2. Blur what you don't want to show. Open the popup and choose Click to blur, Draw area or Blur similar, or scan the page for emails and phone numbers. Press Esc when you're done. Your blurs come back when you return to the site.
  3. Share your screen. With Pro, Presentation Mode turns on by itself when you start sharing in the browser and hides sensitive info in every tab.

Right after you install ScreenShield, the welcome page opens a practice page full of made-up data, so you can try everything safely.

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysWhat it does
Alt+Shift+SStart or stop picking things to blur
Ctrl+Shift+X (Mac: Cmd+Shift+X)Remove all blurs on the current page
Alt+Shift+MTurn Presentation Mode on or off (Pro)
Alt+Shift+HHide the whole screen instantly, and show it again
EscStop picking
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo or redo while picking

Scanning the page and taking a protected screenshot have no keys by default. To add them or change any shortcut, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Free and Pro

Blurring by hand, saved blurs, blur strength, hidden tab titles, the page scan for emails and phone numbers, and the panic key are free with no limits. Automatic detection of 16 kinds of sensitive info, Presentation Mode, your own keywords, black bars, site-wide rules, protected screenshots and backups are part of Pro. See pricing.

Where ScreenShield can't help

  • Chrome doesn't let extensions run on chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store, the built-in PDF viewer or other extensions' pages.
  • Text drawn on a canvas, as in Google Docs or Figma, and text inside images can't be read. Cover them with Draw area.
  • ScreenShield protects what's shown in Chrome, not other apps on your computer.
