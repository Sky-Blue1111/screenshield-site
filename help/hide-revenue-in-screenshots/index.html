Hide revenue numbers before you share a screenshot of a dashboard

Updated November 15, 2026

A screenshot posted in a chat is easy to forward and can't be taken back. Hide the numbers that shouldn't travel, such as revenue, prices or customer counts, before you take it.

Use black bars for screenshots

A blur is fine on a live call, but a screenshot is a sharp image, and a blur can sometimes be undone from one if someone knows the font and size and tries every possible value. A black bar leaves nothing to recover, only the length of what it covers. Open the ScreenShield popup and choose Black bar before you hide anything. Black bars are free.

On the free plan

  1. Open the dashboard in Chrome, then open the ScreenShield popup.
  2. Choose Click to blur and click each number you want to hide.
  3. Use Draw area for a whole chart or panel, and Blur similar for a column of amounts in a table.
  4. Press Esc, then take the screenshot with the tool you usually use. The bars are part of the page, so any screenshot tool sees them.

Your bars are saved for this page and come back the next time you open it. To remove them, choose Clear page in the popup, or press Ctrl+Shift+X (Mac: Cmd+Shift+X).

With Pro

  • Protected screenshots. Choose Take a protected screenshot in the popup. ScreenShield captures the visible part of the page with everything you hid covered by black bars, even if you use blur on screen, and lets you copy the image or download it as a PNG.
  • Money amounts. Automatic detection can find amounts written with a currency sign or code, such as $2,847,392, €1.200 or RM 450. It's off by default. To turn it on, open Settings, go to Automatic detection and check Money amount in the Always column, or in the While presenting column if you only want it during calls. A number without a currency, such as a count of deals or a percentage, isn't an amount, so hide it by hand.
  • Your keywords. Add a client's name or a pattern under Your keywords, and use the test box to check it before you save.

Before you send it

  • Look at the image once before you post it. ScreenShield can't read numbers inside images or charts drawn on a canvas, so cover those with Draw area.
  • If your screenshot tool captures the whole window, the tab title and address bar are in the picture too. Turn on Hide tab title on this site in the popup to replace the title. A protected screenshot shows only the page itself.
