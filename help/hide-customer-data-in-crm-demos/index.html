Hide customer data when you demo a CRM

Updated November 15, 2026

A live CRM demo shows real customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers and deal values to everyone on the call, and to anyone who watches the recording later. ScreenShield hides them in Chrome, so you can show the real account instead of building a fake one.

Prepare the pages you'll show, on the free plan

  1. Before the call, open the list you plan to show, then open the ScreenShield popup.
  2. Choose Blur similar and click one customer name. Every name like it in the list is blurred at once. Do the same for the email, phone and company columns.
  3. Use Draw area for a notes panel or an activity feed, and Click to blur for single fields.
  4. Choose Scan this page for sensitive info to catch email addresses and phone numbers you missed, then Blur all and remember this page.
  5. Press Esc. Your blurs are saved and come back every time you open this page.

Blurs are saved for the address of the page where you made them. If each customer record opens at its own address, blurs on one record don't carry over to the next, so prepare the records you plan to open, or use Presentation Mode.

With Pro

  • Presentation Mode turns on by itself when you start sharing in Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams on the web. It hides email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, keys and tokens in every open tab, including records you didn't prepare, and masks the titles of your other tabs. For desktop meeting apps, press Alt+Shift+M before you share.
  • Your keywords: add your customers' company names, or a regular expression for your account numbers, and they're hidden wherever they appear. Use the test box to check a pattern before you save it.
  • Deal values: open Settings, go to Automatic detection and check Money amount in the While presenting column. Amounts written with a currency sign or code are then hidden during calls.

Good to know

  • If the demo is recorded, choose Black bar in the popup. A blur can sometimes be undone from a sharp recording, and a black bar leaves nothing to recover. Black bars are free.
  • If something private appears, press Alt+Shift+H to hide the whole screen instantly.
  • ScreenShield protects what's shown in Chrome. A CRM desktop app isn't covered.
  • Look over the page once before you share. ScreenShield lowers the risk a lot, but no tool catches everything.
