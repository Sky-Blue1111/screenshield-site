Hide email addresses in your inbox when you share your screen

Updated November 15, 2026

An inbox is a list of everyone who has written to you. Open it during a call to find one message, and everyone watching can read the rest. ScreenShield hides the addresses on the page in Chrome before anyone sees them.

Before the call, on the free plan

  1. Open your inbox in Chrome.
  2. Open the ScreenShield popup and choose Scan this page for sensitive info. The free scan finds email addresses and phone numbers.
  3. Choose Blur all and remember this page. From now on, addresses and phone numbers on this page are hidden every time you open it, and ScreenShield keeps checking as new content appears on the page.
  4. Many inboxes show senders' names instead of their addresses. To hide a list of names, choose Blur similar and click one name. Every name like it in the list is blurred at once, and the blur is saved for this page.

A remembered scan belongs to the address of the page you scanned. If your mail app opens messages or folders at a different address, scan that page too.

Inside a message

The To and Cc lines and the signature often hold more addresses and phone numbers. Run the scan again on the open message, or choose Click to blur and click the lines you want to hide. Press Esc when you're done.

With Pro

  • Presentation Mode turns on by itself when you start sharing in Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams on the web. While it's on, email addresses, phone numbers and other sensitive info are hidden in every open tab, not only your inbox, and the titles of your other tabs are masked. For desktop meeting apps, press Alt+Shift+M before you share.
  • Always hide addresses, not only while you present: open Settings, go to Automatic detection and check Email in the Always column.
  • Your keywords: add a client's name or a project code name, and it's hidden wherever it appears.

Good to know

  • Some web mail apps put your own address in the tab title. Turn on Hide tab title on this site in the popup to replace it, on any plan.
  • If the call is recorded, choose Black bar in the popup. A blur can sometimes be undone from a sharp recording, and a black bar leaves nothing to recover. Black bars are free.
  • If something private appears, press Alt+Shift+H to hide the whole screen instantly.
  • ScreenShield protects what's shown in Chrome. Desktop mail apps aren't covered.
